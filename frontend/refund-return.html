Know your options

Our policy guidelines

Important things to know about different order situations.

📦

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

  • Keep the product and its packaging until the issue is reviewed.
  • Share your order number and photographs, if available.
  • Our team will explain the next steps and any applicable remedy.
🎨

Personalized Gifts

Personalized products are created using details supplied by the customer, such as names, photos, dates and custom messages.

  • Review your spelling, names and custom instructions before ordering.
  • Returns for change of mind may be restricted for personalized products.
  • Errors, defects or other issues will be reviewed under the applicable policy and law.
↩️

Order Cancellation

Contact us promptly if you need to cancel an order. Whether cancellation is possible can depend on its preparation or dispatch status.

  • Customized orders may enter production soon after confirmation.
  • Cancellation requests are not guaranteed once an order is being prepared or shipped.
  • If a cancellation and refund are approved, the applicable process will be communicated.
💳

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the processing method and timing depend on the payment method, payment provider and applicable requirements.

  • Refunds for online payments may be sent back through the original payment method where supported.
  • For Cash on Delivery, an appropriate refund method may need to be arranged.
  • We will communicate any additional information needed to process the refund.
🚚

Shipping & Return Delivery

If a return is authorized, we will explain the return procedure, packaging requirements and applicable shipping arrangements.

  • Do not ship a product back before receiving return instructions.
  • Keep any return shipment receipt or tracking details.
  • Responsibility for return shipping costs must be confirmed under the applicable policy and law.
🧾

Order Information

Having your order details ready helps us investigate issues efficiently.

  • Order number and purchase date.
  • Product name and a description of the issue.
  • Photographs or other supporting information, if relevant.
  • A preferred way for our team to contact you.
