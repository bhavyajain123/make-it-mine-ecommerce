A little transparency goes a long way.

This page describes the intended privacy practices for the MakeItMine storefront. The actual practices must match the services, databases, analytics tools, payment providers and other technologies used by the live website. Replace any incomplete details before publishing.

01 Information We Collect

Depending on how the website is implemented and which features you use, information may be collected when you browse products, create an account, customise a gift, place an order or contact customer support.

  • Contact details: name, email address, telephone number and delivery address supplied by you.
  • Order information: purchased items, quantities, order status, delivery details and gift messages.
  • Personalisation content: names, photographs, text or other material you choose to provide for a customised product.
  • Account information: profile details and authentication-related information, if account functionality is enabled.
  • Technical information: device, browser and basic usage information, where collected by the website or its service providers.
  • Support communications: messages and information you choose to share when requesting help.

Payment information may be processed by a payment provider if a live payment gateway is integrated. The store should not collect or store full card credentials unless it has the appropriate compliant systems.

02 How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate, disclosed purposes such as:

  • Processing orders and delivering purchased products.
  • Preparing personalised gifts according to the instructions you provide.
  • Sending order confirmations and necessary service updates.
  • Responding to customer service questions and resolving order issues.
  • Maintaining account, cart and wishlist features where available.
  • Protecting the store against fraud, abuse and unauthorised activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal, tax and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Improving the website and its shopping experience where permitted.

Marketing communications should be sent only in accordance with applicable requirements and your preferences. Where required, we will request consent and provide a way to opt out.

03 Sharing Information

Personal information should be shared only when necessary for disclosed purposes, with appropriate safeguards and a valid legal basis.

  • Delivery partners: to transport your order to the address you provide.
  • Payment providers: to process and verify payments when online payments are enabled.
  • Technology providers: to host, maintain and secure the website or provide configured services.
  • Professional advisers: where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting or compliance support.
  • Legal authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

This policy does not authorise selling personal information. Any sharing that actually occurs must be accurately described here, including the providers and purposes relevant to the live implementation.

04 Cookies & Browser Storage

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences, understand usage or enable third-party services. The technologies used depend on the final implementation.

This frontend demo may use browser local storage for items such as cart, wishlist or locally saved order information. Browser storage is not the same as a secure, server-side customer database and may not synchronise across devices.

Do not store passwords, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information in plain text in browser storage.

If analytics, advertising or non-essential cookies are added, disclose them accurately and provide any consent controls required by applicable law.

05 Data Security

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure of personal information.

Depending on the deployment, safeguards may include HTTPS, restricted staff access, secure authentication, server-side validation, protected secrets, backups and security monitoring.

No website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. The store should maintain an incident response process and handle any applicable reporting obligations.

06 How Long We Keep Information

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting, dispute-resolution and security requirements.

Retention periods may differ for orders, invoices, support messages, account details and customisation files. A final policy should describe the actual retention approach and how information is securely deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

07 Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, the circumstances of processing and available verification procedures, you may have rights or choices concerning your personal information.

  • Request access to certain personal information.
  • Ask for correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where applicable legal requirements permit it.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Raise a privacy concern or submit a grievance through the designated channel.

To make a request, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

The exact rights, response timelines and complaint procedure must be checked against the laws applicable to the business and its users.

08 Children's Privacy

The store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is not permitted. Where applicable, age-related requirements and parental or guardian permissions must be followed.

If you believe that information has been collected inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law.

09 Third-Party Links & Services

The website may link to third-party websites or use external services such as payment gateways, delivery tracking, fonts or customer support tools. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and practices.

Review the relevant provider's privacy information before using its services. Third-party links should not be treated as an endorsement or as a guarantee of their privacy practices.

10 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the website, its services, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the revised policy should be published on this page with an updated revision date. Where legally required, significant changes should be communicated through an appropriate notice or consent process.

11 Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, your personal information or a privacy-related request, contact the store using the details published on the Contact Us page.
