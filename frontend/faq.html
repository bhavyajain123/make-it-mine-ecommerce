📦 Orders & Tracking

Browse our collection, select the gift you like, choose any available personalisation options and add it to your cart. Proceed to checkout, enter your delivery details and select an available payment method. Follow the instructions displayed on the checkout page to finish placing your order.
Open the Order Tracking page and enter the order details requested there. Tracking information is available only when your order data and tracking service have been connected. For this demo storefront, tracking may use locally saved sample order data.
Cancellation depends on the order's processing stage and whether a personalised product has already entered production. Please contact our support team as soon as possible with your order number. Cancellation eligibility must be confirmed before the order is treated as cancelled.
Visit your account or the Order History page to review available orders. Previous orders will appear only if order history has been saved and connected to your account. A browser-only demo may not sync orders between devices.

🎁 Personalisation & Custom Gifts

Yes, where the product offers those options. Visit our Personalise Your Gift page, select the available customisation choices and provide the requested details. Check your entered spelling and uploaded image carefully before ordering.
If the gift or checkout supports a gift message, you can enter your message in the provided field. Please review the message for spelling and accuracy before submitting your order.
Customisation changes may not be possible once production begins. Contact support immediately with your order number and the corrected details. Changes are not guaranteed until they are confirmed by the store's support team.
Eligibility depends on the store's final return policy and the reason for the return. A damaged, defective or incorrectly personalised item may require a different resolution from a change-of-mind request. Check the Returns & Refunds policy before ordering.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery

Delivery time depends on the product, personalisation requirements, destination and courier. Custom gifts may need additional preparation time. Check the estimated delivery information displayed during checkout and in your order confirmation. Actual delivery dates are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed by the store.
Delivery coverage depends on the store's serviceable pin codes and shipping arrangements. Enter your delivery address at checkout to check whether delivery is supported for your location.
Keep the packaging and take clear photographs of the damaged product and parcel. Contact support with your order number and photos as soon as possible. The store can then review the issue under its applicable damage and replacement policy.

💳 Payments & Discounts

The checkout interface may display UPI, card payments, net banking and Cash on Delivery, depending on the configuration. In the current frontend demo, showing a payment option does not mean a live payment gateway is connected. Online payments require a secure payment provider and server-side verification.
Enter the coupon code in the coupon field at checkout and select Apply. The discount will depend on the configured eligibility rules, minimum order value and any applicable limits. Confirm the final order total before placing your order.
Check your payment app or bank statement before trying again. If money was debited, avoid repeatedly submitting the payment and contact your bank or payment provider. A production store should verify the transaction status through its payment gateway before confirming the order.
Cash on Delivery availability depends on the store's delivery coverage and order rules. If it is enabled for your order, the option will appear during checkout. The current demo interface does not itself verify courier eligibility.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

Contact the support team with your order number, the reason for your request and relevant photos if the item is damaged or incorrect. Eligibility, timelines and resolution will depend on the store's published Returns & Refunds Policy.
Refund timing depends on approval, the original payment method and the payment provider or bank. The support team should confirm the applicable timeline after reviewing your request. Do not consider a refund complete until its status has been verified.

👤 Account & Wishlist

This depends on the checkout configuration. If guest checkout is enabled, you may be able to order without creating an account. An account can make it easier to manage profile details and view saved orders when those features are connected to a backend.
Select the heart icon on a product to add it to your wishlist, then open My Wishlist to view saved products. In this frontend demo, wishlist data may be stored in your browser, so it will not automatically sync across devices.
Visit your Profile page to access available account settings. Changes will persist across devices only when the application is connected to a suitable authentication system and database.
